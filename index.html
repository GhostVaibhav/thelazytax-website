A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
make a promise.

Does a stake mean I put money on the line?

No. Stakes are commitments inside the app. XP and Leaves are virtual rewards; creating a stake is not a bet, cash deposit, or financial investment. Optional paid features are separate from your stakes.

How does a friend become my witness?

Ask your friend first, then select friend verification and enter their email in the app. After you submit proof, they can receive a review request. If a request reaches the wrong person, they can contact us without reviewing the stake.

Why did I receive an email from The Lazy Tax?

It may relate to a requested account action, a witness review, requested beta access, or a support conversation. We explain the types on our email policy page. If you didn't expect a message, email thelazytax@gmail.com.

Can I start using the app today?

The app is currently in testing. Request beta access through Youform to apply to test the app. We'll follow up with testing instructions.
