THE LAZY TAX · YOUR ACCOUNT

Delete your account

You can request deletion by email, even if you’ve uninstalled The Lazy Tax.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email thelazytax@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my The Lazy Tax account.”
  2. Include the email address linked to your account. Send from that address where possible. Never send your password, payment details, or proof photos.
  3. We’ll reply to verify ownership and confirm when your deletion has been scheduled.

Request account deletion

No email app opening? Copy the address above and send your request from any email service. This button prepares an email; it does not submit or delete anything by itself.

What happens next

Once deletion is scheduled, your account has a 24-hour recovery window from the confirmation email. To cancel, use its “Keep my account” link within that window, or sign back into the same account before permanent deletion begins.

After the recovery window, permanent deletion normally completes within the following day. We’ll email you when it’s finished. Once deletion begins, the account cannot be recovered.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, profile name, email address, and account identifier.
  • Your stakes, milestones, proof photos, progress, XP, Leaves, streaks, badges, avatars, and account preferences.
  • Your device notification tokens, witness invitations and links, league membership, and subscription customer profile held by our subscription provider.

What may remain

  • Recovery backups: older database records may remain in recovery backups for up to 35 days after removal from the active database.
  • Usage analytics: user-level and event-level Analytics data uses our two-month retention setting. Standard aggregated reports are not subject to that setting and may remain longer.
  • Crash diagnostics: Firebase keeps crash reports and associated diagnostic identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal.
  • Authentication provider copies: after we delete your Firebase sign-in account, Firebase states that removal from its live and backup systems completes within 180 days. These provider copies do not restore access to your deleted account.
  • Anonymous review outcomes: outcomes on other members’ stakes may remain without your name, email, or account ID. These anonymous outcomes have no scheduled expiry.
  • App-store records: Apple and Google manage their own purchase and billing records under their retention policies. Deleting your app account does not erase those store records.

These provider periods are separate from deletion of your active app data. See Firebase’s retention details for more information.

Your subscription is separate. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Manage renewal in your app-store subscription settings. You can still delete your account while a subscription is active.

You can also request deletion in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account. For other privacy requests, see our privacy policy.
